# Risk Management

This document outlines potential risks identified during the development of the Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) system and the mitigation strategies applied throughout the project lifecycle.

## Identified Risks
Inaccurate Sentiment Analysis - Impact high
Data Quality Issues - Impact high
Model Bias and Fairness - Impact high
Computational Resource Limitations - Impact medium
Insufficient Training Data - Impact high


## Risk Monitoring Approach

The project adopted continuous testing and iterative debugging practices to quickly identify and resolve technical issues during development.


## Mitigation approach

Ensure high-quality training data and continuously evaluate model performance
Implement data validation and preprocessing checks
Conduct bias audits and fairness assessments
Optimise model architecture and parameters
Explore additional datasets and synthetic data generation
